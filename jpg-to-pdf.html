Frequently asked questions

How do I combine multiple JPG files into one PDF?

Select all your images at once (or add them one by one), arrange the order with the arrow buttons and press Convert to PDF. Every image becomes one page of a single PDF file.

Can I convert photos of documents taken on my phone?

Yes. Phone photos of ID cards, certificates, receipts and handwritten notes work well. Choose A4 page size so the PDF looks like a normal scanned document.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is created inside your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

How can I make the PDF smaller?

Choose “Smallest file” before converting, or use our free PDF compressor to reduce it to 200 KB, 500 KB or 1 MB.

Is there a limit on the number of images?

There is no fixed limit. Very large batches depend on your device’s memory; 50+ photos usually work fine on a modern phone or computer.
