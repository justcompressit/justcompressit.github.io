Frequently asked questions

How do I set a full photo as my WhatsApp DP without cropping?

Choose WhatsApp DP, then tap “Fit whole photo (no crop)”. Your entire photo fits inside the square with a blurred or coloured background around it, so nothing gets cut off. Download and set it as your profile picture.

What is the best size for a WhatsApp profile picture?

A square image of at least 500 × 500 pixels works best. WhatsApp shows it as a circle, so keep your face in the middle – the circle guide in the preview shows exactly what will be visible.

How do I post a full photo on Instagram without cropping?

Pick Instagram post (1080 × 1080) or Instagram portrait (1080 × 1350), choose “Fit whole photo” and a background. The whole picture is kept and Instagram won’t crop it.

How do I make a passport-size photo?

Choose Passport 35×45 mm, zoom so your head fills most of the frame and download. Take the photo against a plain white or light background for best results. Always check the exact rules of the office you are applying to.

Will resizing reduce the quality?

Photos are resized with high-quality smoothing. Making a small photo much bigger can look soft, but shrinking a photo keeps it sharp.
