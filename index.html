Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to compress my files here?

Yes. Everything happens inside your own browser. Your images and PDFs are never uploaded to a server, so nobody else can see them.

How do I reduce an image to 100 KB or 200 KB?

Open the Image tab, choose your photo, select the target size and press Compress. The tool tries different quality levels and picks the best one that fits under your limit. If a very large photo still doesn't fit, it shrinks the dimensions slightly too.

How do I compress a PDF to under 1 MB?

Open the PDF tab, choose your file, select “Under 1 MB” and press Compress. For very long PDFs, choosing black & white helps reach smaller sizes.

Why is my compressed PDF not smaller?

PDFs that contain mostly typed text are already small, so there is little to remove. In that case the tool tells you and you can keep the original.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works in Chrome, Safari and other modern browsers on Android and iPhone.

Is it free?

Completely free – no sign-up, no limits on the number of files and no watermark.
