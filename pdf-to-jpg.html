Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PDF to JPG images?

Choose your PDF, select JPG and a quality level, then press Convert to images. Each page appears as a picture you can download one by one or all together as a ZIP.

Can I convert only some pages?

Yes. Type the pages you want in the Pages box, for example “2” or “1-3, 7”. Leave it empty to convert the whole PDF.

Which quality should I choose?

High (recommended) is sharp enough for phones and screens. Choose Very high if you plan to print the images, or Normal for the smallest files.

JPG or PNG – which is better?

JPG files are smaller and accepted everywhere. PNG keeps text perfectly sharp but files are larger.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

Protected PDFs can’t be opened without the password. Remove the protection first, then convert.
