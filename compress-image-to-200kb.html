When to use 200 KB

  • Visa and passport photo uploads – many systems accept digital photos of a couple of hundred kilobytes; always check the exact limit.
  • Website and blog images – faster pages load better for visitors and search engines.
  • Email and chat – send many photos without hitting attachment limits.
  • Marketplace listings – product photos that load quickly on mobile.

Quality at 200 KB

A 200 KB JPG can hold a sharp photo of around 1600–2000 pixels wide – plenty for screens. For web use, try WebP as the output format: it typically delivers the same visual quality at a noticeably smaller size. Read our JPG vs PNG vs WebP guide to choose.

Frequently asked questions

Will compression remove my photo's location data?

Re-encoding the image creates a new file without the original camera metadata, including GPS location.

Which format should I pick?

JPG for maximum compatibility, WebP for the smallest files on websites, PNG only for graphics with transparency.
