About JustCompressIt

JustCompressIt is a set of free tools that make images and PDFs smaller, convert between them and resize photos, so you can use them on social media and upload them to job sites, visa and university applications, government portals and email without hitting size limits.

Why we built it

Many online forms only accept photos under 50 KB or 100 KB and documents under 500 KB or 1 MB. Most people don't have photo-editing software, and many online compressors upload your private documents – ID cards, passports, degrees, bank statements – to their servers.

JustCompressIt works differently: all compression happens inside your own browser. Your files never leave your phone or computer.

What you can do

  • Reduce a photo to an exact size such as 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB or 200 KB
  • Resize photos for passport, ID and job application forms
  • Convert images to JPG, WebP or PNG
  • Shrink scanned PDFs to under 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB or any size you choose
  • Combine photos into one PDF and turn PDF pages into images
  • Resize photos for WhatsApp DP, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and passport size
  • with AI – white, blue, transparent or any colour

The tool is free, needs no sign-up and adds no watermark.
