About CompressKaro

CompressKaro is a free tool that makes images and PDFs smaller so you can upload them to job portals, university admission forms, government websites and email without hitting size limits.

Why we built it

Many online forms only accept photos under 50 KB or 100 KB and documents under 500 KB or 1 MB. Most people don't have photo-editing software, and many online compressors upload your private documents – CNIC copies, degrees, bank statements – to their servers.

CompressKaro works differently: all compression happens inside your own browser. Your files never leave your phone or computer.

What you can do

  • Reduce a photo to an exact size such as 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB or 200 KB
  • Resize photos for passport, CNIC and job application forms
  • Convert images to JPG, WebP or PNG
  • Shrink scanned PDFs to under 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB or any size you choose

The tool is free, needs no sign-up and adds no watermark.
